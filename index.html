About Me

Short stories from my viewpoint about who I am and how I work.

Hello, I’m Christina Lee, an emerging UI/UX designer with a passion for blending the beauty of nature with thoughtful digital experiences. As a soon-to-be graduate, I am eager to grow my skills and deepen my understanding of user-centered design. I prioritize creating intuitive, accessible, and visually harmonious interfaces that resonate with users on a meaningful level. I especially enjoy projects that allow me to incorporate natural elements and simplicity into web design, reflecting my love for the outdoors. My goal is to build a fulfilling career where I can continuously learn, contribute fresh ideas, and craft designs that make a positive impact.

I am passionate about crafting intuitive, accessible, and engaging digital experiences, I am eager to expand my skills in user research, interaction design, and responsive web design.
